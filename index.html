    Returns by Period

    Rolling Returns

    Each point is the yearly (annualised) return over the years up to that day, so it can look back before the From date. It shows how much the result depended on the day you started.

    Holding Periods

    Each line is bought at every month-end in its full price history, whatever the period above, and held for the years shown. Worst, median and best are yearly (annualised) returns. The holds overlap, so they are not independent tries. A dash means the history is shorter than the hold.

    Drawdowns

    Below 0% is below the previous high. The deepest point of each is pinned with its month.
