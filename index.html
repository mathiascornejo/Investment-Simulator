    Statistics

    Volatility, Sharpe, Sortino and beta use daily returns, annualised over 252 trading days. Sharpe and Sortino assume a 0% risk-free rate, which flatters them when interest rates are high. Best and worst year count full calendar years only. Annualised return is shown for periods of a year or more.
